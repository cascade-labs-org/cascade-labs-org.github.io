Privacy Policy — Rentfall

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Rentfall is published by Akash Kalala ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app

collects, why, and how to get rid of it.

The short version

  • Offline play needs no account and sends nothing anywhere.
  • Online, as a guest or signed in, the server holds your display name, your seat and

the current game in memory only, while you are in a room.

  • If you sign in with Google, we keep a small account record so your friends can find

you: your name, profile picture link, a friend code, your friends list and your pending

friend requests. We do not store your email address. Deleting your account deletes

all of it.

  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. Nothing is sold or shared.

What we collect, and why

If you only play offline

Nothing leaves your device. The game runs entirely on your phone, and the app does

not open a connection to our server unless you go to the online screens or have signed in

before. Your settings — sound, vibration, your name — are stored on your own device.

If you sign in with Google (or Apple)

Google (or Apple) sends us a signed token containing an account identifier, your display

name, your email address and (from Google) a profile picture link. We check the signature,

read the identifier, name and picture link from it, and issue our own session token.

We never see your password, and we do not store your email address.

We then keep an account record, so the friends feature works and survives our server

restarting:

Your friends can see your display name, picture link and whether you are online or

playing. Nobody else can look you up: being added as a friend needs your friend code,

which only you can share.

Guest sign-in

A guest account is a random identifier with no personal information attached. **Nothing

about a guest is stored on our servers**, and guests cannot have a friends list. The

guest token lives on your device and is gone if you reinstall the app.

What we do NOT do

  • ❌ No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • ❌ No analytics, telemetry, or usage tracking
  • ❌ No tracking across other apps or websites
  • ❌ No selling or sharing of anything, with anybody
  • ❌ No profiling, and no automated decisions about you
  • ❌ No location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photos — the app requests none of

these permissions, and blocks several explicitly

  • ❌ No chat, and nothing to buy

Crash reporting

This build sends no crash reports. The app contains dormant crash-reporting code, but

no reporting service is configured, so nothing is sent and there is no switch for it. If

that ever changes, this policy changes first, before that version ships.

Where your data is stored

  • On your device: your settings and your session token.
  • On our game server (hosted by Render, in Singapore): rooms and games, in memory

only.

  • In our database (hosted by Neon, in Singapore): the account record above, for

Google/Apple accounts only.

Render and Neon process and store data on our behalf and do not use it for anything

else. Google and Apple are involved only if you choose to sign in with them, under

their own privacy policies.

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. This removes your account record

— name, picture link, friend code, friends list and friend requests — from our database

immediately and permanently, and removes you from your friends' lists. If you are

mid-game, a computer player finishes the game for the others and your name is removed.

By email: akashkalala123@gmail.com, if you no longer have the app. Tell us the display

name you played under and your friend code if you know it (it starts RF-). We do not

store email addresses, so those are how we find the account. We action requests within

30 days.

Deleted records leave the live database at once. Our database provider keeps short-lived

recovery copies for operational safety; they expire on their own within 7 days, and we

never restore a deleted account from them.

Children

Rentfall is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children

under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe

a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete your

data, and to object to processing. Because we hold so little, most of these are

immediate: everything we hold about you is visible in the app (your name, friend code and

friends), and deletion is a button in Settings. For anything else, email us and we will

respond within 30 days.

  • India (DPDP Act 2023): you may raise a grievance with our Grievance Officer at the

address below.

  • EU/UK (GDPR): our lawful basis is performance of the service you asked for —

running the game, and keeping your friends list when you sign in. You may complain to

your local supervisory authority.

  • California (CCPA): we do not sell personal information.

Security

  • All connections use TLS in production (https:// and wss://), and the database

is reached only over an encrypted connection.

  • Session tokens are signed JWTs; a forged one is rejected.
  • The server sends each player only their own cards — nobody else's hand is ever sent

to your device.

  • We never receive or store passwords, email addresses, payment details or location.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top and note it in the

app's release notes.
