RENTFALL

Collect three complete sets. Charge rent. Win.

A fast property-trading card game. Play offline against the computer with no account of any kind, or online - with random players, or with friends in a private room.

  • Three levels of computer opponent
  • Play online with random players, or with friends in a private room

  • No ads. No in-app purchases. No tracking.

How it plays

Ten districts, from the Old Quarter up to Harbour Heights. Collect every card in a district and you have a set; three sets wins.

Each turn you draw two cards and play up to three. Any card that is not a property can be banked as money instead of played for its effect — and deciding which is most of the game.

Rent cards charge for a district you own. An Invoice takes 5M from one player, a Levy takes 2M from everybody. A Snatch takes a loose property; a Takeover takes a whole complete set — unless they play a Veto. Which you can veto back.

Privacy

Very little is collected. Offline play sends nothing at all, and the app does not even open a connection unless you go online. Signing in with Google keeps a small account record - your name, a friend code and your friends list - and nothing else. See the privacy policy.
