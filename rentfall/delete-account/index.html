Delete your account

You can delete your Rentfall account and everything we hold about it, at any time, from inside the app.

In the app

  1. Open Rentfall
  2. Tap Settings
  3. Scroll to Account
  4. Tap Delete account, then confirm

It takes effect immediately, and you disappear from your friends' lists.

What gets deleted

Everything we hold:

  • Your account identifier, display name and profile picture link
  • Your friend code
  • Your friends list and any friend requests, sent or received
  • Your seat in any room you are in

If you are in the middle of a game when you delete, a computer player finishes your game so it is not spoiled for everyone else. Your name is removed from it immediately.

Data kept afterwards

None. Deleted records leave the live database at once. Our database provider keeps short-lived recovery copies for operational safety; they expire on their own within 7 days and we never restore a deleted account from them. The app has no analytics, ads or crash reporter, so there is no other copy anywhere.

Guests and offline play

If you only ever played against the computer, or online as a guest, nothing about you was stored on our side. Uninstalling the app removes what was on your phone.

Cannot reach the app?

Email akashkalala123@gmail.com from any address. Tell us the display name you played under, your friend code if you know it (it starts RF-), and roughly when you last played. We do not store email addresses, so these are how we find the account. We action requests within 30 days.
