TENSPIRE

Ten goals. One winner.

Contract rummy with a ladder to climb. Each hand, collect the next goal on the ladder, lay it down, then race to empty your hand. Finish every goal with the lowest score to win.

Ten goals, in order

Every hand, you try to complete the next goal on your ladder. Lay it down and you move up; miss it and you try again next hand.

  1. 12 sets of 3
  2. 21 set of 3 + 1 run of 4
  3. 31 set of 4 + 1 run of 4
  4. 41 run of 7
  5. 51 run of 8
  6. 61 run of 9
  7. 72 sets of 4
  8. 87 cards of one colour
  9. 91 set of 5 + 1 set of 2
  10. 101 set of 5 + 1 set of 3

This is the Classic ladder. Five more change the climb: Medium and Quick stop early, Evens takes every other goal, and Sprint and Spectrum are made of goals of their own.

Four moves make a turn

  1. Draw

    Take the top card of the draw pile, or the card on top of the discard pile.

  2. Lay down

    Once you hold all of your goal, put it on the table. Part of a goal does not count.

  3. Hit

    With your goal down, add spare cards to any group on the table, yours or anyone else's.

  4. Discard

    Put one card on the discard pile to end your turn. The first to empty their hand wins it.

Wilds stand in for any number or colour. Every set and run still needs one real card.

Skips make a player of your choice miss their next turn.

Scores are the cards left in your hand when someone goes out. Low score wins.

What's in the game

Play your way

  • Offline against the computer, with 1 to 5 opponents and three difficulty levels
  • Online against whoever is around, or with friends in a private room
  • Six ladders, from a five-goal Quick game to the full ten-goal Classic
  • House rules you can switch on and off

Something to come back for

  • Five daily challenges and four weekly goals
  • A free spin every day
  • Achievements that unlock avatars and frames
  • Level and weekly leaderboards, with a board just for your friends

Make it yours

  • Eight decks and tables, from Green Felt to Cosmic Neon
  • 64 avatars in eight families, with rarity frames
  • Everything is earned by playing

Fair and quiet

  • No ads
  • Nothing to buy and no real money: coins and diamonds are earned in the game and have no cash value
  • No account needed to play

Questions

When can I get Tenspire?

Tenspire is in a closed test on Google Play now, and opens to everyone when the test ends. To join the test, email us the Gmail address on your Android phone.

Is it free?

Yes. There are no ads and nothing to buy. Coins and diamonds are earned by playing and have no cash value.

Do I need the internet?

No. Games against the computer play entirely offline. Online tables and friends need a connection.

Do I need an account?

No, you can play as a guest. Continue with Google to add friends and keep your progress when you change phones; progress you made before signing in can come with you.

Is Tenspire on iPhone?

Not yet. Tenspire is on Android first.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the game, open Settings and choose Delete my account. If the game is no longer on your phone, follow the steps on the account deletion page. The privacy policy lists everything the game stores.

Play it before launch

Send us your Gmail address and we will add you to the test on Google Play.
