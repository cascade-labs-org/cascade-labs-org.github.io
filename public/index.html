How we make games

A small studio with a few rules every game we ship follows.

Free, all the way through

Nothing in our games costs money. Coins, decks and avatars are earned by playing, and none of it can be bought.

No ads

No banners, no videos between hands, and no ad tracking.

Offline first

Every game plays against the computer with no connection. Online tables and friends are there when you want them.

Only the data a game needs

Play as a guest without an account. Sign in with Google only if you want friends and your progress kept safe.
