Delete your Tenspire account

Two ways to do it. Both remove everything we hold, permanently.

In the app — immediate

  1. Open Tenspire.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Scroll to Delete my account and confirm.

Your account and everything associated with it are removed straight away, and you disappear from your friends' lists. If you are in the middle of a game, a computer player takes over the hand for the other players and your name is removed from it.

By email — if you no longer have the app

If you have already uninstalled Tenspire, email us from any address and tell us the display name you played under, your friend code if you know it (it starts TF-), and roughly when you last played. We do not store email addresses, so these are how we find the account.

Email akashkalala123@gmail.com

We action deletion requests within 30 days, and usually the same week.

What gets deleted

Everything: your account identifier, display name and profile picture link, your friend code, your friends list and friend requests, your saved progress (coins, diamonds, unlocks, achievements and statistics), and any room or game state holding them.

What we keep, and for how long

Nothing. Deleted records leave the live database immediately. Our database provider keeps short-lived recovery copies for operational safety; they expire on their own within 7 days and we never restore a deleted account from them.

The app has no crash reporter, analytics or advertising, so there is no other copy anywhere to purge.

Guests and offline play

If you only ever played against the computer, or online as a guest, there is nothing to delete on our side — nothing about you was stored. Uninstalling the app removes what was on your phone.
