Privacy Policy — Tenspire

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Tenspire is published by Akash Kalala ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, and how to get rid of it.

The short version

We keep as little as the game needs. We do not sell anything about you, and there are no advertising or analytics trackers in the app.

Playing offline, nothing leaves your phone at all. Playing online needs our game server. If you sign in with Google, we keep a small account record - your name, a friend code, your friends list and your game progress - so they belong to you and follow you to another phone. Signed-in players appear on the leaderboards by first name and initial (you can hide yourself), and online games can be watched by other players. Deleting your account deletes all of it. "What we collect" says exactly what.

What we collect

If you play offline against the computer: nothing. No account, no network, no data leaves your device.

If you play online (as a guest or signed in), we handle:

Data Why How long
Display name So other players can see whose turn it is In server memory while you are in a room
An account identifier To give you the same seat back if you disconnect In server memory while you are in a room
Your cards and score in the current hand To run the game Deleted when the hand ends
That you are watching a game So the players can see how many people are watching (a number - not who) In server memory while you watch

If you sign in with Google (or Apple), we also store an account record:

Data Why How long
Account identifier (a number from Google or Apple, not your email) To recognise your account when you sign in again Until you delete your account
Display name and profile picture link Shown to your friends Until you delete your account; refreshed each time you sign in
Friend code So friends can add you Until you delete your account
Friends list and pending friend requests So you can see and invite your friends Until you, or they, remove the friendship, or an account is deleted
Game progress - coins, diamonds, unlocked items, achievements and game statistics So your progress belongs to your account and follows it to another phone Until you delete your account
Whether you are online, in a room or in a game Shown to your friends while you are connected Not stored - worked out live, and gone when you disconnect
Leaderboard entry - first name and initial, avatar, level, XP, and wins in online games Shown to everyone on the leaderboards, unless you turn it off (Settings → Leaderboards) Until you delete your account

Your friends can see your display name, profile picture link and whether you are online or playing. Nobody else can look you up: being added as a friend needs your friend code, which only you can share.

Leaderboards. If you are signed in, the leaderboards show your first name and the initial of your last name (never your full name or your picture), the avatar you chose in the game, your level and your scores, to anyone using the app. Settings → Leaderboards hides you; you can still see the boards. Guests never appear on them.

Watching games. Anyone using the app can watch a public online game while it is played; a private game can be watched only by friends of a player at it. Watchers see the table, the discard pile, the players' display names and how many cards each holds - never anybody's cards. Players see how many people are watching, not who.

If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive a signed token from them containing a user identifier, your display name, your email address and (from Google) a profile picture URL. We verify that token and read the identifier, name and picture from it - we do not store your email address - then issue our own session token. We never see or receive your password, and we do not receive your contacts, calendar, photos or any other account data.

Guest accounts are a random identifier generated on the spot. Nothing about a guest is stored on our servers: a guest's progress stays on the phone and is lost if you reinstall. Guests cannot have a friends list.

Crash reports

The app does not currently include a crash reporter, and sends no crash data anywhere. If one is added in a future version, this section will describe what it sends - and how to turn it off - before that version is released.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising identifiers, and no advertising SDKs
  • No analytics, behavioural tracking, session recording, performance profiling or crash reporting
  • No location data
  • No contacts, photos, microphone or camera access
  • No payment information — the app has no purchases

Where it is stored

Your session token and a copy of your progress are stored on your device using the operating system's standard app storage. On our servers, room and game data lives only in memory and is gone when the room closes. The account record described above is kept in a managed database hosted by Neon, in the same region as our game server (Singapore).

Who we share it with

Nobody. We do not sell, rent or share your data.

Third parties involved:

  • Google and Apple, if you choose to sign in with them. Their handling is governed by their own privacy policies.
  • Our game server is hosted by Render and the account database by Neon. They process and store data on our behalf and do not use it for anything else.

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings → Delete my account. This removes your account record - your name, picture link, friend code, friends list, friend requests, saved progress and leaderboard entry - from our database immediately and permanently, and removes you from your friends' lists. If you are mid-game, a computer player finishes the hand for the other players and your name is removed from it.

By email: akashkalala123@gmail.com. We will action it within 30 days.

Deleted rows are gone from the live database at once. Our database provider keeps short-lived recovery copies for operational safety; those age out on their own within 7 days, and we never restore deleted accounts from them.

Children

Tenspire is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete your data, and to object to processing.

Because we store so little, most of these are immediate: everything we hold about you is visible in the app itself (your name, friend code, friends and progress), and deletion is a button in Settings. For anything else, email us and we will respond within 30 days.

  • India (DPDP Act 2023): you may raise a grievance with our Grievance Officer at the address below.
  • EU/UK (GDPR): our lawful basis is performance of the service you asked for - running the game, and keeping your account, friends and progress when you sign in. You may complain to your local supervisory authority.
  • California (CCPA): we do not sell personal information.

Security

Traffic between the app and our servers uses HTTPS/WSS. Sign-in tokens are cryptographically verified against Google's and Apple's published public keys before we trust them. We hold no passwords, so there are none to leak.

No system is perfectly secure, but the strongest protection here is structural: we keep very little. No email address, no password, no payment details and no location are stored, so none can leak. The database is reached only over an encrypted connection.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top and note it in the app's release notes.
